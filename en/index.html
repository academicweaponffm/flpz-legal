What FLPZ helps you do

Keep each item and its costs together.

01

Know what you actually earn

Record purchases, extra costs and sales. See actual profit separately from the money still tied up in inventory.

02

Finish the sale

Keep receipts, payment, shipping and refunds with each item. Open tasks and personal reminders help you follow through.

03

Understand your inventory

Search items, storage locations and SKUs, select several categories and sort your inventory. Compare recorded sales while keeping EUR and USD separate.

FLPZ

Tools for your inventory

Check a purchase before buying and find items that have been sitting for a while. Edit several items together and reuse presets for recurring costs.

Purchase check

Estimate profit after costs and fees. See the highest purchase price that meets your profit target, using your own sale-price assumptions.

Inventory radar

Find older active items and see how much capital is tied up in them. Choose your own age threshold.

Personal insights

Find the categories and platforms with the highest average profit, short holding times and your largest cost type. Based on actual sales; group comparisons need three sales.

Fewer repeated entries

Edit several items together, split purchased bundles with cent-exact cost allocation and reuse cost presets.

Keep listings organized

Keep marketplace links, asking prices and status with each item. After selling, see which other listings you still need to remove manually.

Your overview within reach

Optional Home Screen widgets and a weekly stock review. Plus local AI listing drafts on supported devices and matching app colors and icons.

Every feature is free

Manage unlimited flips and use every tool without a subscription or in-app purchase. Sold history, receipts, payment and shipping tracking, refunds, reminders, goals, analytics, backups, CSV and optional iCloud sync are included.

You choose whether to use iCloud

You do not need another app account. Your item records are stored on your iPhone, with personal iCloud sync if you choose it. The app has no ads or tracking SDKs. You enable reminders and widget sharing yourself.

Privacy ↗

FLPZ does not publish listings or access marketplace accounts. AI needs a compatible device and an available Apple Intelligence model; photo analysis also requires iOS 27. Review generated text before sharing.
