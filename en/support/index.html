Support

Questions about FLPZ? Contact the publisher.

Please describe the issue and your app and iOS versions. Do not send passwords, payment details or private records unless necessary.

Features and categories

All features and unlimited active flips are free. Open the category selector in Inventory to choose one or several categories. Search, status and other filters still apply. Sorting includes Category A–Z and Z–A.

Listing assistant

Start with photos and a short description. Include known defects and accessories. Review the draft before sharing. AI works locally on supported devices; a manual template is available without a model. FLPZ does not publish listings to marketplaces.
