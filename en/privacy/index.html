Privacy notice

How FLPZ handles your information.

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Publisher and contact

FLPZ is published by Kiro Laban. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact details below.

Information you add to the app

Your item records, prices, costs, sales, notes, photos, listing links and listing drafts are stored on your device. FLPZ does not require an app account. The publisher does not operate an app server that receives these records. The app contains no advertising or third-party tracking or analytics SDKs.

Optional iCloud synchronization

If you enable synchronization, your records and photos are stored in your personal iCloud account and synchronized between your devices through Apple. The publisher does not receive a copy of your private iCloud records. Turning synchronization off stops future synchronization; it does not delete existing local or iCloud copies. Apple handles its services under its own privacy policy.

Photos, AI and device permissions

FLPZ processes the photos you select and uses Apple’s on-device models for optional AI listing assistance on supported devices. Photos, prompts and generated text are not sent by FLPZ to an external AI provider. Saved drafts may synchronize through iCloud when enabled. System permissions remain under your control in iOS Settings. Optional app lock uses the device’s authentication system; FLPZ does not receive your biometric data.

Purchases and Apple services

Apple processes App Store payments, subscriptions, trial eligibility and refunds. FLPZ uses verified purchase information to determine access to paid features. The publisher does not receive payment-card details. Apple may provide the publisher with sales reports and diagnostics or feedback under Apple’s settings and policies. Apple is responsible for its own data processing.

Exports, backups and sharing

When you export a backup, CSV or listing, you choose where it goes. Files and content shared with other apps, services or people are handled by those recipients. Keep backups private and delete copies you no longer need. Opening an external marketplace link is subject to that website’s own privacy policy.

Support communications

If you contact the publisher, your contact details and the content you send are used to answer and resolve your request. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal records. Support information is retained only as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations. Service requests are handled to provide the requested assistance; other correspondence and abuse prevention are based on the legitimate interest in responding and protecting the service.

This website

These pages use GitHub Pages hosting. We add no analytics, advertising, contact forms, external fonts or tracking cookies. GitHub processes connection information and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, even when they are not signed in. GitHub’s privacy statement explains its retention, international transfers and your options. Links to Apple or other sites only take you there when you choose to open them.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local app records remain until you remove them or the app’s data. Deleted items may remain in backups or recovery copies until those copies are removed. If synchronization is enabled, item deletions synchronize to your other devices; iCloud copies and backups need separate attention. You can manage FLPZ’s iCloud data through Apple’s storage settings. Uninstalling the app or disabling sync does not itself remove iCloud data or exported files. The publisher cannot retrieve or erase your local or private iCloud records for you.

Privacy rights

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information the publisher holds, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on it and complain to your local data protection authority. Contact the publisher about information you sent directly; use Apple’s and GitHub’s privacy channels for information they control. FLPZ makes no automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

Changes

This notice may be updated when the app’s data practices or services change. The date at the top identifies the current version.
