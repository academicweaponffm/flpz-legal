# Writing for FLPZ

The text-editing workflow uses [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer),
version 3.1.0, source `0ef9fe5bc3572734fd3ae6c448fe00a30cd5dcf0` (MIT).

Write for people who buy and resell items. Explain the action and the result
in everyday German or English. Remove filler, inflated promises and generic
sales slogans. Keep sentence length natural and each sentence useful.

Keep supported facts and qualifications. Estimates remain estimates. Clearly
describe manual marketplace actions, free access to all features and unlimited flips and the beta's
availability. Do not add testimonials, savings, ratings or personal stories.

Keep legal meaning, privacy practices, numbers, dates, contact details and link
targets unchanged during a style edit. Review those details separately against
the actual app. Edit the two languages naturally, not word for word.

Humanizer is an editing skill, not a service running on this website. There is
no runtime text API or new account requirement.
